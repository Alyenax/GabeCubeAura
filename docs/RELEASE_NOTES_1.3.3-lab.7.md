This Lab revision makes the Immersive choice immediately visible during guided
setup, even when no audio or Gamescope frame is available.

### Immersive preview

- The real light bar first plays a breathing synthetic Slow Prism using the
  Sapphire palette, then a moving synthetic Screen Sync panorama.
- The Decky preview mirrors the same 17 pixels and identifies the current
  Audio Sync or Screen Sync phase.
- The demo does not depend on live audio, artwork or screen capture and does
  not change the real Immersive configuration.
- Steam ownership, thermal protection and companion-plugin priorities remain
  fully active during the preview.

This remains a private Lab prerelease for target-hardware validation. The
public Stable channel is unchanged.
