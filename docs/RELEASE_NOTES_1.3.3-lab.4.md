This Lab revision tightens preset ordering and makes the first-run choices more
visually representative.

### Presets

- Presets now start with Lights out, Essential and Focus, in that order.
- Lights out explicitly excludes even Valve's download animation. Essential
  still hands confirmed downloads back to Valve.
- Atmosphere now shows Weather at Home. If Weather is unavailable, it falls
  back automatically to its previous Slow Prism Audio Sync display.

### First-run setup

- The four visual choices are Moderate, Atmosphere, Signals and Immersive.
- Signals uses the real two-controller animation during its physical preview,
  even before a controller has been paired.
- Day brightness below `8/255` displays a red warning that the lighting
  experience may be strongly affected.

### Display routing

- Light bar brightness is now the final section, after Home, in-game and the
  optional current-game override.

This remains a private Lab prerelease for target-hardware validation. The
public Stable channel is unchanged.
